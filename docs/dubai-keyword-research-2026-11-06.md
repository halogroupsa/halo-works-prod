# Halo Works keyword research for Dubai

Research date: 6 November 2026

Prioritise fit-out company searches on the homepage, villa and apartment renovation on the renovation page, and joinery company searches on the joinery page. Use office fit-out company wording on the workplace page. This combines Dubai Google Trends signals with Google Ads Keyword Planner search ranges. The company-search phrases have more reported demand than several shorter service phrases. Interior design also has substantial interest, but the website should describe its design coordination role accurately.

## Scope and interpretation

Google Trends was inspected through the browser inside Codex. The main settings were **Dubai subregion (AE-DU), Past 12 months, All categories, Web Search**. All comparisons used search terms rather than topics. English terms were tested; Arabic demand remains outside this research.

Google Ads Keyword Planner was also inspected in the Codex browser with **Dubai, United Arab Emirates, province targeting; English; Google network; October 2025 through September 2026**. Sri Lanka was removed from the research location filter. These are historical estimates for searches in the selected geography, rather than worldwide searches containing the word Dubai.

The website assessment used the current workspace, including its service descriptions, page metadata, journal and campaign page. It is a keyword and content plan, not an audit of the live domain's rankings.

Trends reports relative interest, not monthly search volume, advertising costs or SEO difficulty. Its chart scores use a 0–100 scale within each comparison. A displayed zero can reflect insufficient data. Scores from different comparison sets must not be compared directly. “Top” related queries are searches made in the same search session, not a ranking of every keyword in the market. Rising percentages compare with the previous corresponding period. “Breakout” means growth above 5,000%, which can start from a small base. [Google explanation of related searches](https://support.google.com/trends/answer/4355000?hl=en), [Google explanation of regional interest](https://support.google.com/trends/answer/4355212?hl=en).

The source links below use a rolling 12-month window; later visits may return different values. One year of results does not establish recurring seasonality.

### What changes in the keyword plan

1. **Make fit-out company wording the homepage priority.** Both company phrases show 1,000–10,000 monthly searches, while interior fit out dubai shows 10–100. Cover the variations naturally on one page, without making duplicate pages for singular and plural wording.
2. **Give renovation equal implementation priority.** Villa, apartment, kitchen and bathroom searches each show 100–1,000. Apartment renovation has Medium paid competition, compared with High for the other three. That supports a controlled advertising test, but does not establish lower organic difficulty or better conversion.
3. **Target office buyers through company wording.** Office fit out companies in dubai shows 100–1,000, compared with 10–100 for office fit out dubai. Both belong on the same office service page.
4. **Use joinery company wording alongside bespoke joinery.** Joinery companies in dubai shows 100–1,000, compared with 10–100 for bespoke joinery dubai. Both have High paid competition in these results.
5. **Keep retail and restaurant pages as smaller service opportunities.** Their tested local phrases show 10–100. Do not infer no demand from their sparse Trends data.

Planner reports a year-over-year decline of 90% for several shortlist terms, including fit out companies in dubai, villa renovation dubai and joinery companies in dubai. Apartment renovation dubai and interior design dubai show 0% year-over-year change. Custom wardrobes dubai shows 900% growth but remains in the 10–100 band. These coarse changes do not establish a growing market or precise growth rates. They also differ from the earlier Trends related-query growth signals: the tools use different metrics and reporting windows. Use the Planner ranges to assess demand scale and the Trends findings as separate discovery signals.

## Content priorities

1. **Strengthen the homepage, renovation, office and joinery pages first.** Align each page with the keyword ownership table. Explain inclusions, exclusions, drawings, materials, installation, programme and enquiry requirements. Use verified completed projects when available; the current concept images should remain labelled as concepts.
2. **Expand the existing renovation page.** Add useful villa, apartment, kitchen and bathroom sections before considering separate pages. Split a service only when there is enough distinct scope, imagery, questions and project evidence to justify it.
3. **Develop practical comparison content.** Candidate articles are “How to compare office fit-out quotations in Dubai”, “Bespoke joinery specifications to agree before manufacture” and “What affects villa renovation cost in Dubai”. These are editorial ideas prompted by service intent; their exact titles were not validated as search queries.
4. **Improve existing planning articles.** Connect the renovation checklist, approval-route guide and workplace planning article to the relevant commercial pages. Explain the questions a client should settle before requesting a quotation. Keep any authority guidance current and property-specific.
5. **Validate niche and neighbourhood terms before expansion.** Avoid creating many location pages with interchangeable copy. Community pages need relevant project evidence and useful information about the actual service provided there.

For commercial landing pages, lead with scope, process, project evidence and a clear enquiry route. Cost content should explain the factors behind a quotation; publish numerical prices or durations only when Halo Works can substantiate them.

## Advertising implications

Separate fit-out, office, renovation and joinery enquiries into distinct ad groups. Start with tightly controlled keyword matching and review actual search terms before broadening reach. Route the enquiry to the matching service page or a purpose-built campaign page.

Suggested exclusions for a service enquiry campaign include **jobs, careers, salary, internship, course, degree, training, software, AI tool, free template, DIY, renovation loan** and **home loan**. Review exclusions against actual enquiries so they do not block relevant buyers. Definitions and inspiration searches can be useful for editorial content even when they are unsuitable for a paid lead campaign.

The existing `/fit-out-dubai/` route explicitly uses `noindex, follow` and is excluded from the sitemap. Keep its campaign purpose separate from organic keyword ownership. An indexed service page should carry the organic fit-out target; making the campaign page compete for the same intent would require a deliberate SEO decision.

## Validation and measurement

Refresh the Planner shortlist with the same Dubai province, language and network settings when implementation begins. Exact monthly counts are not available in this account view. Validate Arabic wording with native-language intent research before creating Arabic pages or campaigns.

Track qualified calls, WhatsApp enquiries and submitted briefs by service so that keyword priorities follow actual business results.
